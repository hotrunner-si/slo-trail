Additional information
Arrival and free parking
You can get to Črne njive from Ilirska Bistrica on foot, which will take about 40 minutes, or by car. Just follow the sign for the Sviščaki tourist resort. The road climbs rapidly, first past the church of St. Peter (it stands on a hill and can be seen from far around), bypasses a quarry, and after a good two kilometers of driving uphill, you will see the marking sign of Črne njiva - Brunarica - on the left.
Free parking spaces For private cars, parking spaces are arranged in the vicinity of the log cabin. Please follow the instructions of the directors. In the case of an extremely large number of registered competitors, parking will be possible at an additional location, with arranged free transportation of competitors and companions to and from the venue, at the dates that will be announced on this site.
Road closures
For the safety of the runners, there will be temporary road closures in the area on the day of the event. More information will be published on our site and in local information channels.
Spectators
Friends and family are gladly invited to join you at the Snežnik trails. Viewers can use the same parking lot as runners and can enjoy all activities at the venue of the event. Four-legged friends are also welcome, but please note that there will be many visitors and children at the event, so they should preferably be on a leash.
Volunteers
Snežnik trails simply could not exist without our volunteers. We are always grateful for the reliable hands that help on the tracks, at water stations, as directors, at the venue, when awarding medals, etc..
To help participants enjoy the full atmosphere of the event, please contact us at sneznik.trails@tdbistrc.org . We welcome you to the team. PS. Each volunteer receives a warm meal and a memorial gift.

Competitor's statement - rules of competition
Each registered event participant must read carefully and agree to the rules of the Snežnik Trails event. In them you can find information about registration, restrictions, instructions and other answers to any questions you may have.
By agreeing to the rules or by signing the statement upon registration, the competitor agrees to compete at his own risk and to be familiar with the rules of the competition and the possibility of dangerous situations that may be an integral part of the competition.
By registering, the contestants also agree that the entire recorded film and photographic material can be publicly displayed and used for promotional purposes of the competition. The organizer reserves all the rights to dubbing and distributing the entire film and photographic material. ( Privacy Policy )
Participating with no signed Statement is invalid.
Competition races
Details about the competition courses are available at the links above or on the website TD Bistrc.
Competition routes will be marked (strips, plates with directional signs, flags...), aid / refreshment stations with water and/or snacks will be arranged along the course, in case of predicted hot weather, additional refreshment stations will be added with water.
All competitors must stick to the marked planned routes. Any use of shortcuts and running outside the route is punishable by immediate expulsion from the competition. The directors will also be in key places to help with direction.
Some competition routes cross a local road in certain parts, so compliance with road traffic regulations is mandatory.
The competition will be held in any weather, with the exception of situations where the safety of the participants may be at risk (extreme weather events and other natural disasters). In such a case, the competition's organizing committee decides on the replacement version of the competition courses or even on the cancellation of the competition.
Safety and medical care
Directors, controllers and the rescue service will take care of the safety of competitors on the competition tracks. At any time on the track, the rescue staff, the controller or the director, who assesses that the competitor is no longer able to safely continue the race, can eliminate the competitor from the race, take care of it properly and, if necessary, arrange the transport to the finish area.
If the competitor does not comply with the decision of the rescue staff, the controller or the director, he is immediately excluded.
Recommended and mandatory equipment
For each of the tracks, mandatory and recommended equipment is specified. Read more in the rules of the competition.
Exclusion
The exclusion of a competitor can be in the case of:
disregarding marked competition routes
using shortcuts
using any form of transportation during a match
throwing away or dumping garbage (except at snack bars) and endangering the flora and fauna along the competition track
Replacing the start number with another competitor*
avoiding helping teammates in trouble
unsportsmanlike behavior, insulting teammates, organizer and third parties
disregarding the instructions of the organizer, the rescue service and the controllers
inappropriate content in photos or recordings posted after the competition that insults other participants, the organizer or a third party
For wearing the starting number in the wrong place, 15 minutes (Bistrška 10ka) or 30 minutes (other trails) are added to the final time.
In case of exclusion, after the end of the competition, the organizer will consider the reasons for it and decide on the prohibition of the participation of the excluded person next year or forever, if there are justified reasons for such a decision.

Snežnik Trails Competition History
In 2007, a group of driven running lovers combined their ideas and founded the Bistrc Running Association. In the same year, the 1st Bistrski Runn was organized, which on the first Saturday in June attracted the first distance competitors to popular terrains 10km – on a track we know locally as Bistrska 10ka.
For the tenth anniversary of the Bistrški Run the organizing committee put its heads together and prepared a refresher program: in 2017, the first was held Milanja Trail in two lengths: 17 and 31 km.
Since the tracks run along the beautiful Snežniška Plateau, we made a decision in 2024 to renames the event to Snežnik trails. With this change, the 10-kilometer course got its locally popular name: Bistrška 10, the 17 km course remained the Milanja Trail, and the 31 km course got its name from the highest peak that the runners reach: Kozlek trail. In 2026, we tackled a long-standing desire: the run to the top of Snežnik, the Snežnik trail with a length of 65 km.
In addition to the aforementioned competitions, we organize also youth runs , and for the youngest family members we organize Animation Program .
