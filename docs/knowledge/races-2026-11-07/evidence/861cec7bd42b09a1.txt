FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
REGISTRATION, CHANGES AND STARTER NUMBERS
If I cannot attend the race (due to injury or other reasons), what should I do?
The registration and cancellation policy is available here.
Refunds of the registration fee or transfers to the next event are not possible.
Runners who purchased insurance at the time of registration can independently claim their insurance by providing appropriate documentation (via their myUTMB profile).
Runners who did not purchase insurance at the time of registration may request a partial refund by submitting appropriate medical documentation. Refunds will be processed according to the following timeline:
Until May 1, 2026: 60% refund.
Until August 1, 2026: 30% refund.
After August 1, 2026: No refunds will be issued, regardless of the reason for the runner's inability to participate in the event.
If a runner does not attend the event, they forfeit their race pack and all other benefits granted to participants, regardless of the reason.
I have registered for the race but would like to change the distance (longer/shorter). Is this possible?
Distance changes are possible only until August 18, 2026 , subject to availability. Runners can make the change themselves using their profile (access details are provided in the confirmation email).
After August 18, 2026, distance changes will no longer be possible. No exceptions.
Important: Distance change is only possible if there are spots available. If the race is full, no change of distance can be made.
Can I transfer my registration to another runner?
Yes, transferring your registration to another runner is possible until August 18, 2026 . Runners can complete the transfer themselves in their personal zone via the “Transfer to a friend” option (in the dashboard they accessed through the confirmation email).
A €20 fee applies to each transfer.
After August 18, 2026, runner changes will no longer be possible. No exceptions.
Until when can I register?
The registration deadline is September 3, 2026 , or until the available spots on each course are filled.
If spots are available, registration will also be possible at the event during bib pick-up. The number of available spots will be announced after the registration period closes on September 3, 2026.
Where can I find the list of registered participants?
The list of registered participants will be published after the registration period closes ( after September 3, 2026 **).
Do I need a medical certificate to participate in the race?
A medical certificate is not required. All participants race at their own risk.
When can I pick up my race bib?
Bib pick-up will take place at the Vitranc Hall in Kranjska Gora (Kolodvorska ulica 1a, Kranjska Gora) according to the following schedule:
Thursday, September 17, 2026: 13:00 – 21:00 (for distances: 50km and 120km)
Friday, September 18, 2026: 06:00 – 08:00 (for distance: 50km); 9:00 - 12:00, 15:00 – 20:00 (for distances: 120km and 80km)
Saturday, September 19, 2026: 14:00 – 20:00 (for distances: 25km, 15km and 10km)
Sunday, September 20, 2026: 07:00 – 10:00 (for distances: 25km, 15km and 10km)
BIB number pick-up for the 50K, 80K and 120K races will be organised in time slots.
Runners must book their time slot in advance. You have received an email with a link where you can choose and book your preferred time slot. Please make sure to book your time slot before coming to collect your start number – before 11th September. Limited spots per time slot are available.
Can someone else pick up my race bib?
Runners must pick up their bibs in person (mandatory gear will be checked during pick-up, and participants must sign a liability waiver).
Do the races have start waves? Which one am I in, and can I change it?
Runners who compete on 50k, 25k or 15k races have been divided into two start waves. Runners must respect the division and start in the wave they have been allocated to. Wave change is possible only from wave 1 to wave 2 until August 30th, midnight. Moving from wave 2 to wave 1 is not allowed. The start wave will be marked on the BIB number.
Overall rankings are based on gun time, meaning the first runners to cross the finish line determine the overall winners. If you have been allocated to wave 2 and are aiming for a top overall result, please contact us at info@trailrun.si by 11 September 2026 and provide relevant previous race results. Based on these results, we may exceptionally move eligible competitive runners to wave 1. Requests received after 11 September will not be considered.
Information has been sent to runners via email. You can find a news article with information here as well.
BUS TRANSFER TO THE START AND TRANSITION BAGS
How do I book a bus?
Bus transportation to the start is organized for runners on the 120 km, 80 km, and 50 km distances. You can reserve a seat during registration.
Until August 18, 2026, runners can add a bus reservation by editing their registration (log in, select "edit registration," add "1" under "bus transfer to the start," save, and pay).
Do I have to take the bus to the start?
There are NO parking spaces near the 80 km start in Bled and the 50 km start in Žirovnica. Therefore, we strongly recommend that you book a bus to take you to the start. We have also organized separate bus transportation for accompanying persons so that your family can cheer you on at the start. You can find more information here .
When and where do the buses depart?
Buses depart from the parking lot below the ski slopes (approximately a 15-minute walk from Vitranc Hall) according to the following schedule:
Friday, September 18, 2026 : 06:00 departure to Žirovnica (50 km) - 1st group
Friday, September 18, 2026 : 07:45 departure to Žirovnica (50 km) - 2nd group
Friday, September 18, 2026 : 19:00 departure to Radovljica (120 km)
Saturday, September 19, 2026 : 06:00 departure to Bled (80km)
Runners must be at the bus stop at least 15 minutes before departure.
Is transportation back to the start available after the race?
Yes, runners can book transportation back to their start area. If you are racing the 50K, 80K or 120K and would like to return to Bled or Radovljica after finishing in Kranjska Gora, you have until 18 August to book your bus transfer from Kranjska Gora.
It will not be possible to purchase a bus transfer on-site during the event.
If you are racing the 50K, your bus departure time will depend on your starting wave. We will let you know your exact departure time at the end of August.
As finish times are difficult to predict, buses may fill up quickly. If a bus is full, runners will need to wait for the next available departure. One bus with approximately 50 seats will be available at each departure time.

Transition bags for the 120km distance
Runners on the 120km course will receive one transition bag for the Žirovnica station.
Bags must be labeled with the start number and dropped off at the designated area in Vitranc Hall in Kranjska Gora:
Thursday, September 17: 17:00 – 21:00
Friday, September 18 : 08:00 – 12:00 and 15:00 – 18:00
Bags can be retrieved after the race in Vitranc Hall (same drop-off location) no later than Sunday, September 20, by 09:00.
How does the additional transition bag for women on the 120K and 80K races work?
Additional transition bag will be available for women, competing in 120k and 80k races – it is an optional bag and runners will need to request it at BIB pick up. The transition bag will be delivered to the aid station Sedlo Suha, which is at:
87 km at the 120k race
44 km at the 80k race
This service is part of our initiative to ensure better conditions and support for women in the competition. Hygiene supplies, basic first aid, and a private space will also be available at Sedlo Suha.
Runners will request the additional bag at the BIB pick up and need to drop it off in the Vitranc sports hall :
for 120k race on Friday, 18th September, before 19.00
for 80k race on Friday, 18th September, before 20.00
Important: This is an additional bag and not the same bag as for transition in Žirovnica (120K).
Important: The bag drop off is only in Vitranc sports hall in Kranjska Gora. Bags, dropped off at the start of your race will not be delivered to Sedlo Suha.
Finisher bags (120km, 80km, 50km)
Runners on the 120 km, 80 km, and 50 km distances will receive a finisher bag (for the finish line).
You can drop your bag at the start in the designated van, or at Vitranc Hall during bib pick-up.
Bags must be labeled with your race number. You will receive a sticker together with your race number — place this sticker on your bag.
Bags cannot be left on the bus, as buses do not return to Kranjska Gora.
Bags can be collected after the race in Vitranc Hall no later than Sunday, September 20, by 09:00 .
Do you offer organised supporter bus transfers?
Yes. We provide official supporter bus transfers for the SKY TRAIL 50K, LAKE BLED 80K and I FEEL SLOVENIA 120K races, as well as a free Sunday shuttle bus between Kranjska Gora and Podkoren.
On the dedicated page, you'll find all information about routes, timetables, prices, transfer conditions and how to book your seat.

Aid stations
Where can I find the locations of aid stations?
Information for each distance is available on the website under the "RACES" tab: https://julianalps.utmb.world/races .
What food will be available at aid stations?
Aid stations will provide:
Food : Energy bars, bananas, apples, oranges, tomatoes, cucumbers, soup, cheese, salami, peanuts, chips, cookies.
Drinks : Water, isotonic drinks, Coca-Cola.
At the finish line, a hot meal will be available for all runners (meat/vegan/gluten-free options). Runners on the 120km course will also have a hot meal at the Žirovnica and Dovje stations.
Can I charge my devices at aid stations (120/80km)?
Most aid stations have electricity, except for Tofov Graben and Talež. Runners must bring their own chargers and adapters.
Is sleeping allowed at aid stations?
Some beds will be available at Podhom, Žirovnica, and Dovje.
How long can I stay at the refreshment station?
The stop at the refreshment station is not limited in time, runners should pay attention to the time limits.
Is it possible to charge watches and phones at the 120/80km refreshment stations?
Most stations have electricity – the exceptions are Tofov graben and Talež. Chargers and adapters must be carried by the runner.
Where can I call in an emergency?
The organizer’s emergency number will be listed on the BIB number.
Will there be showers available after the race?
Showers will be available in the Vitranc sports hall (Kranjska Gora).
ASSISTANCE
I want to help a runner during the race. What are the restrictions?
Runners can only be helped at the refreshment station and not between stations, they are completely self-sufficient there.
Assistance is only allowed at certain refreshment stations.
Information on where assistance is allowed and where not, can be found under the section ROUTE DESCRIPTION at each distance: https://julianalps.utmb.world/races/120K .
Will there be a follower race guide?
Information will be available in the “race guide”, which will be prepared for the runners.
Are the refreshment stations accessible by car?
All refreshment stations are accessible by car, except Ratitovec, Golica and Tofov Graben.
Your supporters can book a supporter bus and cheer on you along the trails. More information here .
Will it be possible to watch the race live?
Yes, there will be live streaming via the Europe Trail Cup Facebook profile.
Will runners be geolocated?
The 120km and 80km runners will have GPS trackers. Online monitoring will also be possible – the link will be published in the week before the event.

Where can I find the ».gpx« files of the routes?
GPX files for individual distances are available on the “ROUTES” tab: https://julianalps.utmb.world/races . The final files will be uploaded at the end of August, as small changes are still possible.
Is the route marked?
The routes are marked with orange stripes, flags with reflectors visible in the night and boards. We recommend that runners download the GPX file to their watch before the race (make sure it’s the final course version), just in case of sabotage or removal of course flags and markings.

ITRA points
The match does not earn ITRA points.
As part of the UTMB World Series, Julian Alps Trail Run by UTMB® follows the UTMB Index system and does not award ITRA points.
However, ITRA operates independently and may publish race results from UTMB World Series events on its platform, allowing runners to receive an ITRA Score.
Please note that the publication of results and the assignment of ITRA Scores are entirely managed by ITRA. As an event organiser, we do not have a direct collaboration with ITRA and cannot guarantee that results will be included or updated in their database.
If your race results or ITRA Score are missing, we kindly recommend contacting ITRA directly.
MANDATORY EQUIPMENT
Where can I find a list of mandatory equipment?
The list of mandatory equipment is available here .
The organizer reserves the right to make changes in case of exceptional weather conditions.
Each runner must have their own reusable cup.
Mandatory equipment check.
Runners must bring all mandatory equipment to the BIB pick up. The inspection will be carried out at the time of BIB number pick up.
Mandatory equipment can also be checked at the start or on the course. It is the duty of the runners to have the mandatory equipment with them from the start to the finish line.
What happens in case of incomplete mandatory equipment?
Each runner must show the mandatory equipment if required. For each missing piece of mandatory equipment, the runner gets 0.5 hours extra. If a runner refuses to show equipment, he is disqualified.
Running poles
If runners choose to take hiking poles with them, they must carry them for the entire duration of the race. It is forbidden to start without poles and get them during the course of the race. No poles are authorized in the spare bags.

MEDAL ENGRAVING
What is medal engraving?
Laser engraving is a service that puts your name, surname, official finish time, distance, and elevation gain in metres directly on your finisher's medal. The process takes less than a minute.
What exactly will be engraved on my medal?
Name · Surname · Official finish time · Distance (e.g. 80K) · Elevation gain (e.g. 3826 m+). All data is linked to your race number.
How much does engraving cost?
Pre-order (until 16 September 2026): €10. At the EXPO during the event: €15.
How do I pre-order engraving?
Pre-order at engraving.witisi.com/jat2026. Pre-orders are open until 16 September 2026.
I didn't pre-order. Can I still get engraving?
Yes. Visit our tent at the EXPO in Kranjska Gora during the event (18–20 September). Bring your race bib for identification.
Is engraving available for all distances?
Yes — we offer engraving for all distances: 10K, 15K, 25K, 50K, 80K and 120K. Each medal receives the actual data from your specific race.
When and where is the engraving tent?
Our tent is located at the EXPO in Kranjska Gora throughout the event (18–20 September). Find the exact spot on the EXPO map.
