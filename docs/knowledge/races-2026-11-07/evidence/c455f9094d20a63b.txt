SKY TRAIL 50K
You’ll start in Žirovnica village, the cultural capital of Slovenia, and the route will take you across peaks, offering stunning views all the way until the finish line in Kranjska Gora.

Route description
The 50 km route starts in Žirovnica village and then takes you across peaks until reaching the finish line in Kranjska Gora. There will be many stunning views of the Julian Alps and of the Austrian mountains and valleys as well.
You’ll begin your race from the village Žirovnica, cultural capital of Slovenia . Few meters higher, you can see lake Bled, with an island in the middle. Then your route takes you to the highest point of your race – Karavanke ridge , and can already be seen from Završnica valley. Don’t forget to take some pictures from the highest point of your race, because it’ll offer breathtaking views of Julian Alps and Mt. Triglav (highest peak of Slovenia).
You are following the ridge right to Golica and Klek . From there you have a great downhill in store, taking you straight to Mojstrana. A flat section follows next, but you still have some climbing to do – climb to the Srednji vrh , which offers a really nice view and then return to Kranjska Gora.

STARTING BLOCK INFO
All runners have received an email with the information about which starting block they have been assigned to.
Please make sure to check this information and strictly observe the time limit indicated at the top of this page during the run.
In the TIME CHARTS, the time limits for the second starting block are listed.
Runners, when planning to check out the route, please make sure you’re only using marked paths and are mindful of warning signs about wood harvesting. Be careful when crossing pastures and be mindful about animals’ behaviour. Always keep your dogs leashed when crossing such areas. Make sure the only print you leave on the trails is the one coming from your shoes. Pick up your trash and always carry poop bags for your dogs.
Mandatory equipment
In order to ensure security and the smooth running of each event, each participant must carry with them all the items listed in the mandatory equipment (see below). And take all the equipment with them during the race.
According to the weather conditions, the organization might require runners to carry additional “hot-weather” or “cold-weather” kit and will inform each participant before the opening of the BIB collection.

Registration
Maximum number of runners is 1200.
The registration is final once we receive your payment.
In case bus transfer is needed from Kranjska Gora to start of the race additional charge 15 EUR will apply.
IMPORTANT :
The 50-kilometer race will take place in two waves on Friday, September 18, 2026. The first wave begins at 8:00 AM , followed by the second at 9:30 AM . Runners will be informed of their assigned group ahead of the race.
Group registrations
Minimum number of runners is 5 (from whichever distance).
Group registration price per runner is 15% (10 km, 15 km, 25 km) or 10 % (50 km, 80 km, 120 km) lower than individual registrations.
In case of group registrations do not register into the system until you get a group registration code from us . You first have to fill out this table , which you can download to your computer, send it back to us via e-mail ( info@trailrun.si ) and then you’ll receive all further instructions.
All runners receive

Finisher’s medal
A meal in the finish area
