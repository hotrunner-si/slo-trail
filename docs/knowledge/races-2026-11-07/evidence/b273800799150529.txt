Welcome to the 4th Trail po kra&scaron;kih klancih!
Dear running enthusiasts!
The
&Scaron;RD Kras &ndash; Run for Life team
invites all running enthusiasts to join us for the
4th Trail po kra&scaron;kih klancih, taking place on 7 March 2027 in Križ near Sežana. Awakening spring nature, varied gravel roads and forest trails, a friendly atmosphere, excellent organisation and authentic Karst hospitality await you!
See you on the KARST HILLS! 🙂

PLEASE NOTE
THE LONGER COURSE (19.5 km) COUNTS TOWARDS THE 2027 PRIMORSKA MOUNTAIN RUNNING SERIES (PGT).
!!! THE TIME LIMIT FOR THE 19.5 km COURSE IS 2 HOURS AND 45 MINUTES. !!!
Both courses follow forest trails and gravel roads, with a short section on asphalt.
PRIZES
Every runner who crosses the finish line will receive a unique finisher medal.
The overall fastest finishers on both courses will receive trophies and a gift bag.
The top three finishers in each age category will receive medals.
POST-RACE REFRESHMENTS
After crossing the finish line, you can look forward to a hot meal, delicious homemade pastries and a relaxed get-together with fellow runners and supporters.
RACE-DAY REGISTRATION
Registration will also be available on race day; however, please note that a race pack cannot be guaranteed for race-day registrations.
The race-day entry fee is &euro;50.
To guarantee your race pack, we recommend registering in advance.
IMPORTANT!
Changing your course or transferring your race entry to another person is FREE OF CHARGE until 20 February 2027. After this date, changes will temporarily be unavailable.
On race day, this service will be available for a fee of &euro;5 per person. Please arrange any changes at the information desk.

RULES:
The organiser's emergency telephone number, to be used in the event of an accident or injury, is printed on the race bib.
Each participant takes part at their own risk and confirms this by accepting the electronic declaration during registration.
By registering for the race, each participant confirms that they are physically fit to take part and understand that they are responsible for their own safety.
The organiser accepts no liability in the event of an accident or injury.
All participants must follow the marked course. Taking shortcuts is strictly prohibited and will result in disqualification. The use of any means of transport along the course is prohibited.
If you have not seen any course markings (ground markings, ribbons or arrows) for more than 200 metres, return to the last visible marker and check the possible routes again.
The organiser reserves the right to reproduce and distribute all video footage and photographic material from the event for promotional purposes and for use in articles and on social media.
How to get to the event venue:
1
Option
Enter Križ, Sežana into your GPS. The first parking option is located immediately at the entrance to the village, near the church (in the car park next to the cemetery and on the nearby grass area). The event venue is approximately 200 metres from the parking area.
2
Option
The second parking option is located at the Village Hall, where the event will also take place. For this location, enter Križ 2 into your GPS.
3
Option
If you do not have or use GPS, take the motorway towards Sežana and exit at Dane. Immediately turn left towards Sežana, then at the next junction turn right towards Nova Gorica. After approximately 5 km, you will arrive in Križ.
4
Option
You can also take the Senožeče motorway exit. After leaving the motorway, immediately turn right towards &Scaron;torje. Continue straight through &Scaron;torje towards Sežana and, at the junction, turn right towards Nova Gorica.
Please arrive at the event in good time to avoid crowds, complete your registration
and prepare for the start without rushing.
Children's run for preschool and primary school children
The children's course is 400 metres long and is non-competitive. Every child will receive a medal and a surprise bag upon registration.
Participation is free of charge, and younger children may be accompanied by their parents or guardians.
Advance registration is recommended by email at: gfabjan3@gmail.com
The children's run starts at 9:30.
