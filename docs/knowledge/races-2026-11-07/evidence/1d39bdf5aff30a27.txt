Equipment
GENERAL
In order to ensure security and the smooth running of each event, each participant must carry with them all the items listed in the mandatory equipment (see below). And take all the equipment with them during the race.
According to the weather conditions, the organization might require runners to carry additional “cold-weather” kit and will inform each participant before the opening of the BIB collection.
120K
MANDATORY EQUIPMENT
Running backpack (or other carrying equipment suitable for attaching a timing chip) intended to carry the mandatory equipment and which must be worn throughout the race)
Closed trail running shoes with tread profile outsole
**GPS tracker **
The GPS tracker is provided by the organizer. The GPS tracker must be in the correct place and remain switched on at all times. Loss of the tracker results in immediate disqualification.
Smartphone
To ensure constant availability before, during, and after the race, the runner must:
have international roaming enabled and functioning in the country where the event takes place.
have the organization’s safety phone numbers saved.
use a visible (unblocked) phone number.
start the race with a fully charged battery.
keep the phone switched ON and be available at all times (airplane mode is forbidden and subject to penalty).
have the LiveTrail application installed and activated.
it is recommended to carry an external battery (powerbank).
Collapsible cup 15 cl/5 oz minimum (Bottles or flasks with lids are not accepted!)
Visible BIB number
Bib number must be in front and visible at all times. Running without a visible BIB number or losing it during the race will result in immediate disqualification.
Min water supply of 1 L
2 working headlamps + spare cells pack (Recommendation of 200 lumens or more for the main torch light!)
Whistle
Food reserve (Recommendation of 800kcal (ex: 2 gels + 2 power bars))
Survival blanket or bag (Dimension of survival blanket: 1.40m x 2m minimum / 55 inches x 80 inches)
Jacket with hood
The jacket must be suitable for severe weather conditions on mountainous terrain. It must be made of a waterproof* and breathable** membrane (e.g. Outdry)
*Minimum recommended waterproof rating: 10,000 water column (10,000 mm).
**Recommended breathability value: RET 13 or lower (lower value = more breathable).
The hood must be integrated or attached using the manufacturer’s original system.
The seams must be fully taped.
The jacket must not contain any panels or sections made of non-waterproof materials.
Manufacturer-designed ventilation openings are permitted (under the arms, on the back).
Basic first aid pack (Self-adhering elastic bandage for bandaging or strapping (minimum 100 cm x 6 cm))
Long-legged trousers (OR race leggings OR a combination of leggings and socks to cover the legs completely)
Cap, bandana or multifunctional neck gaiter designed to protect the head from the sun, windy and cold weather
An additional warm second layer
A warm long-sleeved mid-layer (non-cotton), weighing at least 180 g (6,5 ounces), OR a combination of warm long-sleeved base layer (first or second layer, non-cotton) weighing at least 110 g (4 ounces) and a windproof jacket* with durable water-repellent (DWR) treatment.
Note: the windproof jacket does not replace the mandatory waterproof jacket, and vice versa!
Warm gloves

Cold weather kit
120K & 80K
In case of cold weather, the cold weather kit will be activated and can contain:
durable water repellant (DWR protection) windproof jacket
hat
warm and water-proof gloves
waterproof overpants
3rd warm layer (intermediary layer between the 2nd layer and the waterproof jacket)
Recommendation: fleece or compressible down jacket
light running gaiters
50K

an additional warm second layer (A warm long-sleeved mid-layer (non-cotton), weighing at least 180 g (6,5 onces), OR a combination of warm long-sleeved base layer (first or second layer, non-cotton) weighing at least 110 g (4 onces) and a windproof jacket* with durable water-repellent (DWR) treatment. The windproof jacket does not replace the mandatory waterproof jacket, and vice versa!)
